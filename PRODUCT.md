# Product

## Register

marketing

## Users

**MCO and payer contracting directors** evaluating a transportation management vendor. They skim for credibility, compliance posture, network capacity, and a pilot path, and they decide in minutes.

**Transport provider owners in the DMV** deciding whether joining is worth it: what is required, how claims and payment work, whether this company is organized. The site is also their genuine how-to resource for becoming a compliant provider in DC, MD, and VA.

**Facility staff and case managers** confirming this is a serious partner for the people in their care.

**Members**, lightly: reassurance and what to expect. No consumer sign-up exists.

## Product Purpose

The site is the company's representative before any human conversation. Its job: make an evaluator conclude, within one visit, that Nexo Access is a serious, well-built medical transportation management organization that delivers trips through a credentialed provider network and answers for every one of them, on a platform that enforces what others merely promise. Success is measured in credibility per minute, not clicks.

## Brand Personality

Premium, calm, credible, regional. Mobbin-class visual ambition: richly animated, modern-technology-company confident, but every claim evidence-backed and every motion budget-governed. Healthcare-adjacent trust: plain language, no hype, honesty gates on every string.

## Anti-references

- Consumer taxi language ("book your ride today") anywhere near the lead.
- Framing that disowns transportation ("we don't drive", "we're just software"). We manage the service and we own the outcome.
- Legacy broker sites' visual clutter and dated chrome. Their information depth is the reference; their design is the anti-reference.
- Generic AI-SaaS slop: purple gradients, identical stat-card grids, decorative glass on everything.
- Any site that renders differently on a cheap monitor than on a designer's machine.

## Design Principles

1. **Static-complete first.** SSR, no-JS, and reduced-motion always see a finished page; animation is layered on top, never load-bearing.
2. **Consistency is a feature.** One site on every machine. Divergence is a defect unless deliberately decided and recorded.
3. **Motion within budget.** Mobbin-tier ambition, old-device-proof by measurement.
4. **Evidence voice.** State what the platform enforces, nothing the code cannot back.
5. **Depth over gloss.** The MCO director and the provider owner both find their answers without asking.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA minimum per nexo-brand's non-negotiables: text at 4.5:1 or better and boundaries at 3:1 or better against real composited backgrounds; full reduced-motion paths; keyboard and screen-reader correctness. The member audience skews elderly and low-vision, so this is identity, not compliance.
