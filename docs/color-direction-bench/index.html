D24 impact check, written before building as the law now requires

Planned work: render alternative colour directions for an owner ruling. No site code, no invariant touched, no token changed.

Collisions mapped:

· nexo-brand §1 tonal law: under review by owner ruling, which is legitimate. §1 is a decision, not a physical constraint. It is being questioned, not violated.

· D23 perception floors: PORTABLE, and they bind every direction here. The A06/B06/E1-07/E2-05 separations were measured as perception thresholds, not as jade-specific values. D3's warm ink pair was re-tuned to #110b09 / #32211c (1.27) precisely because the warmer, prettier pairs measured 1.17–1.23 and fell under the shipped 1.25. Floors-only-rise applies whatever the hue.

· Status-colour semantics, the collision that cannot be argued, only seen. Red means refused; amber means deadline. A warm brand puts the identity in the alarm's own hue neighbourhood. Every direction therefore renders a real status row, D3 included and unflinched.

· D21 type: untouched. this bench dogfoods the shipped tokens.

· Railway receipt (§13): a new ruling would refine it with a reversal receipt, per the founding law. Not discarded.

· W2 hero concepts: synergy. an atmosphere layer feeds that work.

Unresolvable by bench → owner forks: whether the warm thesis (neither fully medical nor fully technology) outweighs the semantic cost quantified below; and how much atmosphere is "enough" before it competes with the copy.

Credit, as asked

The atmosphere technique in D2 and D3 is GitLab's, read from about.gitlab.com and dissected in docs/DESIGN_RESEARCH.md §14a: layered CSS radial-gradients with alpha falloff, anchored off-canvas so the viewer sees the falloff and never the hotspot. It is six declarations, zero bytes, zero JS, and it does not animate. What we add: the hue geometry (D2's warm family is jade's complement, chosen so the two harmonise rather than compete), the AA discipline under every text zone, and the D23 floors applied to a palette that is not ours yet.

How to read this: the protocol

Read it on BOTH machines (the good monitor and the standard laptop). The whole reason D23 exists is that the second machine disagrees with the first.

React by direction number. You are not picking a hex; you are picking a direction. The ruling becomes D25: adopt, synthesize, or hold.

The layers can be split. S-012 records three of them (atmosphere, composition, card treatment) and you can adopt any one without the others. D2 is precisely "atmosphere only".

Until you rule, the hardened jade palette remains law. Any adoption then goes through a full token-implementation task with the cube and AA re-verification, honouring floors-only-rise.

The one question the bench cannot answer for you: whether the warm thesis is worth the status-semantics cost. The bench renders the cost; the judgement is yours.
