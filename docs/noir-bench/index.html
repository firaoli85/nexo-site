THE NOIR SELF-CHECK, answered honestly.
What sings: the braid at route scale with one live line is the clearest idea in the whole programme. It reads as precision routing rather than as decoration, which is exactly the brand thesis, and it is the first fragment where the subtle Blade Runner edge and the medical-transport job stop fighting each other. The network band and the braid coexist, because one is structure and the other is a path through structure.

What I am least sure of: whether the mint survives being the ONLY colour. Stripped of the warm dusk, the page is ink, charcoal, white and one green. That is genuinely a high-end developer tool, and it is also one step from cold. N3 is where you decide whether "clinical" is the compliment or the criticism.

Where it does not hold: the composite is still three strong voices (nav, braid, cards), and Chanel's rule was ratified but not yet applied. If the braid is the signature, the cards should get quieter than they are here: the neon border is a second neon event competing with the live strand. My recommendation: the mint border belongs on ONE surface, and the braid has the better claim.
